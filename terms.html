Terms of Service

Last updated: 24 November 2026

"Video TikTok Aura" is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by its owner to prepare short videos and send them as drafts to the owner's own TikTok inbox through the TikTok Content Posting API.

1. Use

The tool is used only by its owner, with the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. No automatic publishing

The tool never publishes content by itself. Every video stays a draft until the owner reviews it in the TikTok app and publishes it manually. AI-generated content is labeled as such at publication.

3. Content

Videos use only royalty-free or public-domain images and respect TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

4. Liability

The tool is provided "as is", without warranty. The owner may stop using it, or revoke its access from TikTok settings, at any time.
